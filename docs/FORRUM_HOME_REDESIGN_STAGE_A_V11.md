# FORRUM Home Redesign — Stage A V11

Stage A introduces the approved dark visual foundation **only for the homepage**.

## Included

- soft graphite page background;
- dark surface hierarchy for the existing three homepage columns;
- restrained low-contrast borders;
- primary white and secondary grey text hierarchy;
- green-turquoise functional accent;
- dark hover, focus and selected surface tokens;
- existing homepage controls are made readable on dark surfaces without changing their structure or behaviour.

## Explicitly not included

- header redesign;
- header navigation changes;
- news unread indicator;
- collapsed category sidebar;
- category-tree structural changes;
- replacement of the current feed with the two approved tables;
- 24-hour discussion ranking changes;
- new News editorial block;
- curator / section proposal block;
- footer redesign;
- responsive redesign.

These are later approved stages and are intentionally not bundled into Stage A.

## Anti-AI rules

No gradients, blur, glassmorphism, glow or decorative shadows are introduced by this stage.
